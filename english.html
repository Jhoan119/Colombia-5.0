What is Colombia 5.0?

Colombia 5.0 is the most important technology innovation event in the country, organized by the Ministry of Information and Communications Technologies (MinTIC). It brings together entrepreneurs, developers, designers, students and companies in a unique space for knowledge and exploration of the Colombian digital ecosystem. Its goal is to drive the TIC Mission 2035, the Colombian government's roadmap to consolidate the country as a digital powerhouse in Latin America.

Open Hardware Definition

VI Hispalinux Congress · 2003

Open hardware offers the same 4 freedoms as free software, but applied to hardware designs: schematics, PCB and manufacturing files. This allows anyone to study, modify and manufacture their own devices.

PCB Schematic Manufacturing

Technical Glossary

Key terms from the digital ecosystem presented at Colombia 5.0. Understanding them is essential to grasp the country's technological transformation.

Artificial Intelligence

Technology that enables machines to learn, reason and make decisions similar to humans. Central theme of Colombia 5.0 as a driver of the country's digital transformation.

AI

Open Hardware

Electronic devices whose schematics and designs are publicly available and freely modifiable. Applies the 4 freedoms of free software to physical components like PCBs and circuits.

Open Hardware

Digital Animation

The process of creating moving images using specialized 2D or 3D software. Colombia has positioned its animation industry internationally with productions like Escape India and Underdogs United.

Creative Industry

FLISoL

Latin American Free Software Installation Festival. A community event promoting the use and development of open technology. At Colombia 5.0 it presented the Open Hardware conference.

Free Software

MinTIC

Colombia's Ministry of Information and Communications Technologies. Organizer of Colombia 5.0 and responsible for the country's digital policies toward the TIC Mission 2035.

Digital Government

TIC Mission 2035

The Colombian government's roadmap to consolidate the country as a digital powerhouse in Latin America. It sets goals for connectivity, digital talent and technological innovation by 2035.

Digital Policy

Conclusion

Colombia 5.0 proved that the country has the talent, creativity and technology to lead Latin America's digital transformation.

Honestly, I didn't know what to expect from Colombia 5.0, but the event surprised me. Seeing Colombian animation projects with international quality up close, and learning about open hardware, made me realize that technology isn't just for big companies or developed countries. There are people in Colombia doing incredible things, and that's motivating. I leave with more drive to keep learning and the feeling that I chose the right path.
